About the web demo

The demo is the real app, running entirely inside your browser. Your Instagram ZIP is opened, parsed and stored locally — it is never sent anywhere, and there is no account or server behind it. Loading this page downloads the app from GitHub; after that it works offline.

A browser can't offer everything a phone can, so the demo leaves out the Android-only protections: app lock, encryption at rest, screenshot blocking and the home-screen widget. Those toggles are hidden rather than shown doing nothing. For the complete app, install the APK.

Prefer nothing hosted at all? Run it on your own machine downloads a 1 MB bundle — unzip it and double-click run-mutual.cmd (Windows) or run-mutual.sh (macOS/Linux). It serves the app from your own computer, so not even the page comes off a server. Needs Node.js or Python, which most machines already have.
