Mutual — run it on your own computer
====================================

Everything in this folder runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, and the
app makes no network calls of its own.


To start
--------

Windows      double-click  run-mutual.cmd
macOS/Linux  run          ./run-mutual.sh   in a terminal

Your browser opens at http://localhost:8321. Leave the small black window open
while you use Mutual; closing it stops the app.

You need either Node.js (nodejs.org) or Python (python.org) installed — most
computers already have one. The launcher checks for both and tells you if
neither is found.


Why not just double-click index.html?
-------------------------------------

Opening the file directly gives the page a "file://" address, and browsers
refuse to give those pages a database. Mutual would start but could not
remember your import. Served from http://localhost it gets real, persistent
storage — which is the whole point.


Using it
--------

1. In Instagram: Settings -> Accounts Center -> Your information ->
   Download your information. Choose JSON format.
2. Wait for Instagram's email and download the ZIP.
3. In Mutual, click Import and pick that ZIP.

Your data is parsed in the browser and saved in that browser's local storage.
It never leaves your computer.

To install it like a real app, click the install icon in your browser's
address bar ("Install this site as an app"). It then gets its own window and
taskbar icon.
